# Poultry Farm Data Analysis

## Overview
Analysis of 8 months of operational data from a poultry farm in Abuja, Nigeria (Aug 2024 – Mar 2025).

## Business Questions Answered
1. Which month was most profitable and which was worst?
2. Are feed costs eating too much into revenue?
3. What is the egg production trend?
4. Is there a pattern in bird mortality?

## Key Findings
- **Best month:** March 2025 — ₦77,000 profit
- **Worst month:** January 2025 — ₦18,000 profit
- **Feed costs** average 74% of revenue, leaving a thin 26% margin
- **Egg production** is inconsistent across the 8 months
- **Mortality spike** observed during harmattan season (Oct–Jan), likely due to cold weather stress

## Recommendations
- Consider producing feed in-house to reduce costs
- Provide heat sources for birds during harmattan season
- Current flock productivity is strong — scaling bird count could increase output significantly
